Sudoor (صدور)

The IndoPak 15-line mushaf for iPhone and iPad, with Arabic recitation and the Urdu translation read aloud. Free, with no ads and no accounts. Created by Salman Ahmed.

Help

  • Turn pages: swipe. Tap a page to show the navigation.
  • Hear an ayah: double-tap it (or press and hold), then choose Play Ayah, From Here, or اردو for the Urdu.
  • Urdu translation: tap اردو beside a surah, then Play. Drag the panel's handle to move it; double-tap the handle to put it back.
  • Settings (gear on the home screen): page turn, Larger Text, Urdu audio in the app or streamed, reciter, Credits.
  • Siri: "Recite Ya-Sin in Sudoor", "Play Al-Mulk in Urdu in Sudoor", "Continue reading in Sudoor".
