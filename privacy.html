Sudoor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Sudoor is a free Quran reader created by Salman Ahmed. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. Sudoor does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. We do not track you.

What stays on your device

Your settings, bookmarks and the last page you read are saved only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

To play a recitation, Sudoor downloads the audio for each ayah from EveryAyah (everyayah.com). These requests contain only the name of the audio file; like any website, EveryAyah's servers can see your device's internet address. No other data is sent.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Live Activities

These features run on your device using Apple's system services. Sudoor receives nothing from them except the action you ask for.

Children

Sudoor is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
